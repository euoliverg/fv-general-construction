Six trades. One crew.

We build the structure and we finish it. You don't have to coordinate a framer, a siding sub, and a deck builder. One crew carries the job from sheathing to the last nail.

  1. Framing

    New home framing

    Floors, walls, and roofs framed plumb, square, and tight, then sheathed and ready for every trade that follows.

    • Wall, floor & roof framing
    • Dormers
    • ZIP System sheathing
    • Basement framing

  2. Additions

    Additions & remodeling

    More room without moving. We take down what's tired, frame what's new, and tie it into the house so it looks like it was always there.

    • Home additions
    • Demolition
    • Renovations

  3. Siding

    Siding

    Cedar shingles, cedar clapboard, and vinyl, laid in straight courses with tight corners and clean trim lines.

    • Cedar shingles
    • Cedar clapboard
    • Vinyl siding

  4. Trim

    Trim & finish carpentry

    Casings, moldings, corner boards, and the interior details that make a room read as finished.

    • Interior trim & moldings
    • Exterior trim
    • Corner boards & rakes

  5. Decks

    Decks & porches

    Decks built to be used: solid framing underneath, clean fascia, steps, and built-in seating where you want it.

    • Composite & wood decks
    • Porches
    • Built-in benches & steps

  6. Windows

    Windows & doors

    Replacement and new-construction windows and doors, flashed and trimmed to keep New England weather outside.

    • Window replacement
    • New-construction windows
    • French & entry doors
